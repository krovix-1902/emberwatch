03 · How it works

Six steps that make two satellites comparable

01 One schema

Both sensors to one table; MODIS 0–100 confidence mapped to VIIRS low/nominal/high.

02 Grid, not pixels

Detections binned to a 0.25° grid. Raw point counts never compared across sensors.

03 Cell-days

The unit is one cell, one day, any burning. Near-invariant to pixel size and platform count.

04 A shared floor

Both sensors clipped to 8.4 MW, the weakest fire MODIS reliably sees.

05 Calibrated on overlap

Median VIIRS-to-MODIS ratios per cell and month, fitted on 2013–2025.

06 Then tested

Plotted across the join; the residual step is measured. That test is the next slide.

The key move is counting cell-days instead of raw detections, so pixel size and how many satellites are flying stop distorting the record.

06 · Honest limits

What it can’t tell you

  • Detections are not burned area. One large fire and several small ones can count the same.
  • Cloud and overpass gaps hide fires. A quiet pixel is not a certified absence of fire.
  • Industry burns too. Gas flares and volcanoes give some cells a permanent false fire season.
