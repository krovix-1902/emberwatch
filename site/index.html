What this does not tell you

  • Hotspot detections are not burned area. A single large fire and several small ones can produce the same count.
  • Cloud, smoke and overpass gaps cause missing observations, not absent fires.
  • Thermal sensors also see gas flares, volcanoes and industry, so some areas show a permanent false fire season.
  • Scaling factors fitted on few overlap years are weak. Every value carries the number of years behind it.
